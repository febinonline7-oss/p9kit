# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps: being welcoming and patient with beginners, accepting
constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the project, showing empathy.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: harassment of any kind, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer
privately through the contact details on the repository. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated, and will result in a response judged necessary and appropriate. The maintainer is
obliged to keep the identity of the reporter confidential.
